Prevention and Safeguards

Reduce Human Error

One major cause of data breaches as reported by IBM is human error making up “nearly half” of all reported data breaches.

- Enforcing strong password policies

- Use role-based access control (RBAC) and two-factor authentication (2FA)

- Train users on proper data handling practices

Secure Infrastructure

- Apply the same level of protection to both main and backup databases

- Maintain consistent end-to-end encryption for all data transmissions

- Conduct routine security reviews and audits

- Maintain compliance with national standards

Prepare Breach Response Protocols

- Secure operations physically and digitally by taking services offline

- Establish a data security team or contact a hired third party

- Consult legal counsel and notify law enforcement

- Notify affected parties
